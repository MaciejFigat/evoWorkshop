## Passport: Warsaw Streets

Kind: game
Core: none. A feel experiment for the break-time arcade: the outcome is decided by the player's execution (movement, attack timing, blocking), so there is no path to embedding
Source of randomness: seeded PRNG from the host for enemy side, cooldowns, attack choice and camera shake; the deal is the run, replayable from the seed and the input log
Player decisions: movement, attack chains, kick, special, jump, block; all execution skill
Removed from the original: nothing; original design with original art after the concept sheets
Kept: the whole arcade loop: waves, camera lock, lives, score
Round format: individual; one run is one round, 2 to 5 minutes
Business model: play money only, no stakes; internal break-time game, no licence category
Embedding level: C presentation only
Red flags: appeal to children through the arcade look; execution skill keeps it out of gambling
Free when porting: theme, art, sound, pace, input. Requires recertification: nothing, there is no paytable
