Sprite extractor

Cuts the animation frames out of the concept sheets and builds the transparent atlases the game loads. Runs entirely in the browser; no Python, no server.

Select (or drop) all the files from assets/reference/ (at least the ones named in manifest.js), then save the downloads into assets/sprites/ (the .png) and data/ (the atlas_*.js), replacing the old ones.

Drop the reference images here, or click to choose them
